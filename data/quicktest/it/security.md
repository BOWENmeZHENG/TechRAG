# Account and Device Security

*Fictional sample document for the TechRAG demo.*

## Passwords and sign-in

Use the company password manager and a unique password of at least 14 characters for every
account. Multi-factor authentication is required for all systems, using a hardware key or an
authenticator app. SMS codes are not allowed.

## Laptops

Disks must be encrypted, and the screen must lock after five minutes of inactivity. Install
operating system updates within seven days of release.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT help desk within one hour of noticing. IT will
lock and wipe the device remotely and reset your sessions.

## Access requests

Request access to a system through an IT ticket. The system owner approves it, and unused access
is removed after 90 days.
