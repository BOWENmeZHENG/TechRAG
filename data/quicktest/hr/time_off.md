# Vacation and Sick Time

*Fictional sample document for the TechRAG demo.*

## Vacation

Full-time employees accrue 20 vacation days per year, at 1.67 days per month. Up to 5 unused days
carry over into the next year and expire on March 31. Request vacation in the HR portal at least
two weeks ahead for absences longer than three days.

## Sick time

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, usable for their own illness or to care for a family
member. Tell your manager before your shift starts. A doctor's note is needed only for absences
longer than three consecutive days.

## Company holidays

The company observes 11 paid holidays. The People team publishes the dates every November.
