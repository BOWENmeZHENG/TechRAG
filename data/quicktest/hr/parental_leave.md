# Parental Leave Policy

*Fictional sample document for the TechRAG demo.*

## Who is eligible

Full-time and part-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment. The policy covers
birth, adoption and foster placement.

## Length and pay

Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of parental leave at full pay. Part-time employees are paid
in proportion to their scheduled hours. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or
placement, in one block or in up to two blocks of at least two weeks each.

## Requesting leave

Submit a request in the HR portal at least 30 days before the leave starts. If the date cannot be
known in advance, tell your manager and the People team as soon as you can.

## Returning to work

For the first four weeks back, employees may work 80% of their usual hours at full pay. Health
benefits continue throughout the leave.
